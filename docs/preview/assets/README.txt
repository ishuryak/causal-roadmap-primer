ROADMAP ILLUSTRATIONS

illustrations/ contains four approved, separate PNG sketches adapted with AI
assistance from Andy Wilson's original hand-drawn figures. These are raster
assets, not editable vector diagrams. Each is 1448 by 1086 pixels.

- cause.png: one observed treatment world and an unknown counterfactual.
- context-and-missingness.png: the data-generating process, including recording.
- dag-and-plan.png: L -> A, L -> Y, A -> Y, Y -> R. There is no L -> R arrow.
- inference-engine.png: question, data and assumptions support a conditional
  identification decision, followed by estimation or revisiting the plan.

The figures are displayed separately in index.html, with descriptive alt text,
scientific captions and links to the full-size originals. Keep the captions
consistent with any future revisions. They state proposed assumptions, not
estimated causal effects or a claim that Susan's data identify her target.

The optional clinical examples use the hollow-headed patients, indigo and
green pen marks, and purple arrows of Andy Wilson's ECA teaching cartoons.
The two WebP illustrations are web renditions of the AI-assisted drawings
prepared with Andy for the November 18 roadmap review.

- trial-or-records.webp: Susan considers a new trial and existing records.
- same-cohort.svg: the same illustrated patients under each hypothetical
  schedule. The figure reuses the same three drawings in each branch.
- time-zero-and-frailty.webp: Susan can align dates in her charts. An
  unrecorded pretreatment confounder requires other evidence or sensitivity
  analysis.

EARLIER EDITABLE CONFOUNDING SKETCH

context-confounding.svg and context-confounding.drawio retain the earlier
three-node example. They are no longer the main-flow illustration in step 2.
The draw.io source has separate nodes, labels and connected arrows. Open it
in diagrams.net and choose File > Export as > SVG, retaining a readable
viewBox and text, to revise the vector export. Keep arrow directions and
descriptions consistent with the accompanying argument.
