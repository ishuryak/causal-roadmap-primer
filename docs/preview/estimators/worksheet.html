Paper notation

X is baseline frailty. W is the schedule (0 standard, 1 short). Y(w) is the outcome under schedule w. In Eqs. 7–8 the superscript on Yi or Xi labels a record from that arm.

μ is mean risk, τ a short-minus-standard effect and D̃ a signed pseudo-outcome. A hat marks a fit. Eq. 9 allows any weight g(x) between 0 and 1. Here it is the short-schedule propensity at that frailty.
