Self-test · for medical students and residents

Thirty questions on causal diagrams

These questions follow the optional section of the causal roadmap primer on causal diagrams (directed acyclic graphs, DAGs). They are short scenarios from oncology, critical care, emergency medicine and perinatal research, and six ask you to read a diagram and choose what to adjust for. After each answer you see why it is right and the source. The final report lists your misses and links each one back to the subsection of the primer that covers it.

Questions and answer letters are shuffled on every start. While you are in a question, keys A to E or the arrow keys pick an answer and Enter on an answer submits it. Links and buttons work as usual: Enter on a source link opens it and never scores or skips a question.
