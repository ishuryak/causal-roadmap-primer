CAUSAL ROADMAP PRIMER V2
Live: https://ishuryak.github.io/causal-roadmap-primer/v2/#roadmap
This is the illustrated edition with Bleile checkpoints, November 12, 2026.

Edit these files directly. index.html contains the primer, bleile-model.mjs
contains the provisional rubric and verdict rules, bleile.mjs connects the
checkpoints and profile, and bleile.css styles them. story.* supplies the
illustrated reading layout. The existing scientific examples live in app.mjs,
sims.mjs, trial_argument.mjs and dag_paths.mjs. No build is required for Pages.

From the repository root: node tests/test_bleile_v2.mjs
The main edition remains at ../ and links here. Future edits to v2 should be
made in docs/v2, not regenerated from another copy of the primer.

The fictional 60-to-50 example preserves positivity at 8 while improving
confounding, measurement and sensitivity. Equal totals can yield different
verdicts. Dings are a teaching rubric, not an empirical probability or ranking.
Profile links contain the ten scores and evidence-basis flags only.
Introduce the roadmap first, then gamification, then the score. The total and
worked verdict appear only in that roadmap exercise. There is no floating
score or repeated score panel in the final interpretation section.
